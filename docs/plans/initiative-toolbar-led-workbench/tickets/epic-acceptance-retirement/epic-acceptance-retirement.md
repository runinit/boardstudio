# Accept all workspaces and retire the old dock

## Workflow

Installed BMad skills govern orchestration, ticketing, review and verification. Legacy sources and historical notes retain behavioral requirements and evidence, not their proof-map, mutation, fixed-review or ordered-closure protocols. Functional acceptance and explicit human/phase approvals remain unchanged.

## Description

After the five adapters are complete and the user authorizes M3, perform the six-workspace responsive, keyboard, contrast, motion, packaged and adapter acceptance. Retire superseded dock paths only after parity is demonstrated, preserving unrelated preferences, actions and source-defined behavior.

## Outcome

BoardStudio users receive the complete six-workspace toolbar-led experience, with the old dock retired only after the required acceptance evidence and user approval.

## Requirements

R1 (parent R1): Verify the integrated six-workspace shell and global actions delivered by M1/M2 through 007's packaged acceptance; preserve them during 008 retirement. Host and adapter implementation remain owned by the preceding epics.

R5 (parent R5): Check the completed M2 adapter inventory dispositions and evidence at 007's integrated acceptance boundary, then preserve those capabilities through 008 retirement. Reuse sufficient owner/admission receipts; adapter implementation and its focused proofs belong to M2.

R6 (parent R6): Preserve 007's M1/M2 prerequisites and 008's dependency on 007. Begin this epic only after completed adapters and explicit M3 authorization; acceptance must precede retirement.

R7 (parent R7): Record concise evidence for accepted behavior in the BMad ticket plan, distinguishing executed, reused and unrun checks; no separate proof-map protocol is required.

R8 (parent R8): Use installed BMad skills for implementation, review and verification. Explicit human and phase approvals remain required; report unmet criteria and follow-ups without waiving approval.

R9 (parent R9): Carry M1's applicable Layout/global-header receipts and limitations into 007, reusing unchanged evidence while adding the later adapters' acceptance. Do not retroactively treat M1 evidence as proof of M2 behavior; the M1 candidate and hands-on stop remain owned by the Layout epic.

R10 (parent R10): Old dock paths and related state are retired only after all six adapters have parity and M3 acceptance is complete; preserve unrelated preferences, project/Export navigation, save honesty, legacy geometry access and existing data/geometry/output semantics. Demonstrate parity and retained behavior before retirement.

R11 (parent R11): 007 owns final six-workspace width/zoom, keyboard/focus/touch, rendered contrast, packaged visual parity and reduced-motion acceptance against the architecture literals, incorporating sufficient M1/M2 receipts. 008 preserves those outcomes through retirement; frozen mockups cannot substitute for real-app evidence.

## Constraints

- M3 implementation waits for all M2 adapter work and the user's explicit authorization; approval of this split authorizes planning only.
- Reuse sufficient M1 evidence with receipts; do not claim M1 proves the five later adapters or repeat unchanged checks without need.
- Retire shell mechanisms only after six-workspace parity. Keep frozen HTML/README sources unchanged and do not retire legacy scripting or unrelated shared primitives.
- Create only the two assigned legacy ticket identities 007 and 008; no new acceptance/refactor tickets.

## Done when

- The user has authorized M3 after all adapters are complete, and the two assigned entries retain their source gates and original dependency order.
- 007 demonstrates the named width, touch/focus, rendered contrast, packaged visual parity, adapter integration and reduced-motion criteria across all six workspaces, reusing unchanged M1 receipts with limitations stated.
- 008 demonstrates parity, preserved preferences/commands, proof retention, shipped documentation and the final stable integrated gate before retiring old paths.
- Follow installed BMad skills for implementation, review and verification; explicit user and phase approvals remain required.

## Boundaries

- Touch point: Six-workspace responsive/keyboard/contrast/motion/packaged and adapter acceptance — entry 007; M1 Layout/global-header receipts may be reused where unchanged.
- Touch point: Parity-gated dock path/state retirement, documentation and final full gate — entry 008.
- Must not change: Frozen HTML/README snapshots, unrelated preferences, legacy geometry-script access, domain behavior or acceptance criteria without user approval.

Trace: M2 completion → user authorization for M3 → 007 six-workspace acceptance → 008 retirement and final integrated gate.
