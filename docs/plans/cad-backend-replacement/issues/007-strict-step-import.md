### Closure rules shared by every package

- **Unit of completion.** The finite unit is an **owner path**: every branch the runtime actually executes, from original source selection to emitted mesh/wire arrays. Each geometric stage terminates exactly once as proved semantic identity, complete bounded repair, or refusal.
- **Three distinct terms.** *Semantic identity* is a source-defined conversion with the same finite image. Examples are §4.5.19 placement-axis normalization and a positive affine knot relabel. *Actual repair* moves a support, selects a different finite image or changes a filled region. *Numerical/approximation* error comes from decimal→binary64 conversion, evaluation, or chord/facet tessellation. Numerical or approximation Unknown does not imply repair, and a small residual does not make a repair numerical. Arbitrary numerical error need not be charged as movement, but a real geometric term cannot be removed by calling it numerical.
- **Missing capture is implementation work, not ambiguity.** Solver failure proves neither mathematical ambiguity nor impossibility. Several source-consistent finite selections require an authenticated selection or a conservative whole-domain correspondence covering every admissible choice. No corpus over-budget repair and no irreducibly ambiguous source selection has been established.
- **Unknown is asymmetric.** [014](014-vendor-mesh-collision-validation.md) may keep unsupported *analysis* Unknown. Under 007 final strict policy, unproved geometric *repair* refuses. Unchanged and numerical-only routes may keep explicit Unknown analysis/approximation bounds.
- **Delivered and kept.** These stay delivered: native LINE vertex-chord/attachment enforcement, captured immediate spline mutations, the fully clamped same-master planar seam and the narrow complete cuboid-zero class. Reuse their controls and extend only their concrete composition gaps. None of them lends authority to unproved source trim or master selection.
- **Current gap.** The [importer ledger](../../../../cad/monstertruck/src/step/repair.rs) and [IO report](../../../../cad/monstertruck-qualification/vendor/monstertruck-io/src/step/load/convert/repair.rs) still insert incomplete original-selection/master/surface coverage.
