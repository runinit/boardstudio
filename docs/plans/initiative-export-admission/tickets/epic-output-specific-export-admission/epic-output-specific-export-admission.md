# Output-specific export admission

## Workflow

Installed BMad skills govern orchestration, ticketing, review and verification. Legacy sources and historical notes retain behavioral requirements and evidence, not their proof-map, mutation, fixed-review or ordered-closure protocols. Functional acceptance and explicit human/phase approvals remain unchanged.

## Description

Deliver output-specific availability and click admission across the existing Export flow. ZMK establishes the seam first; PCB/design-file and geometry-package admission reuse it independently. Preserve the frozen tickets' complete requirements and acceptance obligations through the adopted source companions.

## Outcome

Designers see honest blockers for each output and can click eligible exports through the real application to receive a current artifact or precise failure, while ownership and incomplete-design persistence remain intact.

## Requirements

R1 (parent R1): ZMK availability and click admission agree on the accepted board and physical instance's known firmware prerequisites, including reviewed controller profile, assignments, supported split roles, transport and applicable peripherals. Demonstrate the real firmware transfer or precise blocker, asynchronous failure and corrected-plan retry.

R2 (parent R2): Ordinary and Draft KiCad, SVG, DXF and footprint-library availability reflect their distinct selected-board PCB, resolved wiring, contours and definition prerequisites. Demonstrate ordinary transfer, Draft findings, export-owned preparation, unrelated-edit refusal and package-failure retry without blocking independent valid outputs.

R3 (parent R3): Keycap STL, authored Case STEP and generated mechanical packages use their distinct accepted-scene, authored-case, physical-instance, mount and exact-generation prerequisites. Demonstrate each named output or precise blocker/provider limitation; preview meshes are not exact manufacturing authority and preview refresh alone does not revoke a valid captured export.

R4 (parent R4): A retained control cannot dispatch or deliver for another board, physical instance, editor session, accepted revision or executor. Demonstrate changes between display, click and asynchronous work, including same-ID/revision reopen, with no wrong-source artifact, stale delivery or false success; a fresh row reflects its current owner without requiring messaging on a detached row.

R5 (parent R5): Incomplete split designs remain saveable, reopenable and editable; only outputs depending on missing hardware are blocked. Demonstrate visible output-specific reasons and unrelated valid exports, without fabricating missing hardware or a global completeness error.

R6 (parent R6): Acceptance clicks the real mounted Export rows through Runtime/Session with viable accepted fixtures and controlled browser adapters, inspecting named artifacts and format/content or exact reported limitations. Demonstrate blocked rows and failure paths; fabricated ready booleans, direct callbacks and native-only tests do not replace mounted click evidence.

R7 (parent R7): Captured source ownership, independent Runtime source/worker revalidation, Session admission, leases, active-work checks, output-builder validation and final-delivery checks remain separate. Demonstrate still-current delivery after operation settlement, overlapping current firmware artifacts with only the newest relevant status, stale rejection and browser download failure reported distinctly from generation failure.

R8 (parent R8): Preserve exactly 001 ZMK export admission, 002 PCB and design-file export admission, and 003 Geometry-package export admission. Demonstrate 001 as the first admission-seam slice, with 002 and 003 depending on 001 and independent of each other; preserve legacy ready status as ready-for-dev plans without claiming implementation completion.

R9 (parent R9): Each ticket extends the relevant existing regressions, runs changed native and browser targets, and records commands/results at closure. Demonstrate source-specific artifacts, blockers and ownership at the mounted boundary, supplemented by focused native Runtime/Session/Core/provider evidence.

## Constraints

- Apply the parent's constraints to every entry: Available is eligibility to begin, not guaranteed delivery; no universal readiness bit or forwarding façade. Retain strict captures, accepted document authority, independent Runtime/Session/lease/builder/delivery checks and precise provider limitations.
- Source companions remain the detailed behavioral and testing contract; shared R4–R7 and R9 apply to each entry's own output family. 001 owns the seam and overlapping firmware semantics; 002 owns ordinary/Draft preparation/retry; 003 owns exact-versus-preview and preview-refresh behavior.
- Candidate mismatches in research need realistic evidence; do not promote them all to demonstrated production defects.
- This documentation migration authorizes no implementation, additional story or sweep. The ready-for-dev status records legacy readiness, not completed proof.

## Done when

- In the production Export flow, real mounted ZMK, ordinary KiCad, Draft KiCad, SVG, DXF, footprint-library, keycap STL, authored Case STEP and generated mechanical row clicks run through Runtime/Session with viable accepted fixtures and controlled browser adapters. Inspect each named artifact's format/content or precise provider/changed-source failure; neither a ready boolean, directly invoked callback nor native-only evidence substitutes for these clicks.
- Mounted blocked rows show their specific missing prerequisites and keep unrelated valid outputs actionable. Retained controls across board/instance changes, same-ID/revision session reopen, accepted-source changes and executor replacement dispatch nothing stale, deliver no wrong-source artifact and report no false success; fresh rows reflect current owners, with no detached-row messaging requirement. Incomplete split designs remain saveable, reopenable and editable.
- ZMK proves reviewed profile, assignments, split roles, transport and applicable peripheral admission, genuine firmware-request rejection, corrected-plan/failure retry and delayed/source-changing preparation. Two still-current overlapping exports can both deliver while only the newest relevant status wins; final delivery works after active operation settlement, and browser download errors remain distinct. Captured ownership, Runtime source/worker revalidation, Session admission, leases, in-progress checks, builders and final delivery keep their independent checks for every family.
- PCB/design-file clicks preserve ordinary wiring rejection versus actionable Draft findings, selected-board contours and footprint definitions, multi-board identity, refreshed plans, export-owned accepted preparation/protection and capture advancement only for that export, unrelated-edit refusal and package-failure retry without abandoned operations blocking the next attempt.
- Geometry clicks distinguish accepted keycap mesh outputs from authored Case exact STEP and generated mechanical packages, preserving selected-instance/mount initialization, accepted-scene currency and generation findings. An unchanged-source export survives disposable preview refresh; accepted-source/executor changes reject stale delivery, and download failures remain separately visible.
- Extend relevant regressions and run changed native/browser targets, recording newly run versus reused results in the BMad plan; no child status alone establishes epic acceptance.

## Boundaries

- 001 owns ZMK and the output-specific seam across presentation, Runtime/Session, firmware construction and browser delivery.
- 002 consumes 001 and owns ordinary/Draft KiCad, SVG/DXF and footprints; retain wiring, preparation, protection, package and retry owners.
- 003 consumes 001 and owns keycap, authored Case STEP and generated mechanical admission; retain exact CAD, mount, generation and provider owners.
- Touch point: Shared Export presentation and Runtime/Session lifetime contracts remain coordinated through 001's seam. 002 and 003 have no dependency on each other's output families; shared-file edits require coordination, not an invented prerequisite.
- Out of scope: Portable project export, Export styling, Layout architecture, universal completeness validation, missing providers, geometry rewrites, fabrication guarantees or replacing Session/lease/final-delivery authority.
