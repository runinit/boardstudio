## Question

without changing its cases, assertions, real packaged-provider inputs or failure behavior?

  port files in `research/chromiumoxide-web-split`, including freshness and failure paths.

  package and negative controls; distinguish observed behavior from assumed protocol semantics.
- Record an equivalent-or-blocked decision with evidence and remaining limits. Only register a
  replacement after equivalence is established; do not remove coverage to obtain a timing.
- Preserve unrelated work and use isolated build targets. No push, merge or hosted CI.

## Answer

Resolved on 2026-10-29 **only for the local face-replacement decision**: the adapted browser
regression and its experimental gate integration have sufficient focused parity and failure-control
evidence for the approved sequence to proceed to [19](19-node-free-ergogen-oracle.md). The
implementation is on `research/chromiumoxide-web-split`, not promoted to the production gate.
This resolution does not certify a complete browser step, full gate, package provenance, Node-free
process tree, or performance. Those integrated checks remain blocked and tracked in
[22](22-node-free-gate-verification.md). Historical blocked/claimed statements below describe their
dates and are superseded only for this local decision.
