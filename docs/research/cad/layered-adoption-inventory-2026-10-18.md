## Remaining implementation boundaries and decision

1. **006/013 cone-wall events:** retain the supplied fixture; design analytic varying trim/event handling in existing `layered/{contours,topology}.rs`. The simple half-cone is a lower-bound repro, not permission to support only wall-centred axes. No stepped geometry or general Boolean requirement.
2. **006/013 inward-shell ownership:** preserve all material components and assign sealed voids to their actual owners in `topology::assemble`; use the round and rectangular fixtures plus nested/multiple-owner controls. Bounds-only or arbitrary first-outer assignment is insufficient.
3. **006/013 multi-ring void union:** retain both positive/negative planar Boolean outputs with correct role/ownership in `contours::difference`; the U+cap island remains connected through its floor. Preserve cut order and source lineage. Point contacts still need valid topology rather than blanket acceptance.
4. **006/013 PCB cutter composition:** validate original outline/hole ownership first, then apply complete round/oval cutter unions/intersections with their authored identities. Source holes are ownership declarations; drill/slot operands can intersect each other or the outer boundary. Keep raw source and placement semantics. No converter/mesher, vendor STEP or shared analysis-contract changes belong to this work.

**Actual user decision needed:** none to retain the already-authorized geometry in the seven fixtures. Their inputs already fit the public source/PreparedCaseIR representation. A proposed feature exclusion or manufacturing restriction would need explicit user approval; none is adopted. Generic imported-PCB CaseBody modifiers are not a demonstrated product path; if that broader composition is requested, first establish its semantics with the parent instead of dropping fields or changing public contracts incidentally.
