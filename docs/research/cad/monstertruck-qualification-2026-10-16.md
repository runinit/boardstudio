# Monstertruck replacement: first executable qualification gates

Measured 2026-10-16. This follows the [feature assessment](monstertruck-feature-assessment-2026-10-16.md) and preserves the requirement to remove OCCT/C++ completely from the eventual CAD backend, build and required tests. The [qualification crate](../../../cad/monstertruck-qualification/README.md) is isolated from production. It is an executable feasibility harness, not a backend switch.

## Decision

**Begin the approved production port alongside qualification repairs.** The user approved production/removal slices005–012 and directed replacement to start now; the earlier recommendation to defer broad porting is superseded. The shipped geometry kernel currently remains Cadrum while callers move to verified Monstertruck code. The approved self-crossing and exact support-owner validation policies are implemented in Core and direct CAD. Keeping the application working during migration does not establish the final architecture: complete OCCT/C++ removal remains mandatory.

## Goal execution flow

The user's2026-10-17 scope decisions require exact layered geometry and STEP for generated cases and PCBs only.013 includes PCB outline plus drilled holes at thickness;006 adopts it and closes demonstrated gaps.009 is mesh-only appearance/optional printing;004 exact cutters and general exact Boolean hardening are off the critical path. Preserve their frozen evidence and integrated fixes. The [existing map](../../plans/cad-backend-replacement/map.md) controls current dependencies; broader exact-keycap/general-Boolean requirements later in this historical record are superseded, not newly waived test failures.

Vendor STEP uses tolerance-bounded meshes for display, alignment and basic collision validation, retaining original bytes, source geometry/face information and complete occurrences. Units/placements and incomplete-import rejection remain mandatory; missing units default to millimetres. Repairs remain bounded by applicable authenticated source precision. On2026-10-17 the user approved supported tolerance bands by default and refinement on demand, with0.01 mm as an explicit tighter check.014 reports intersection, clearance/contact locations and bands, including uncertain outcomes when unsupported. No analysis accuracy is inferred from a display mesh. [007](../../plans/cad-backend-replacement/issues/007-strict-step-import.md) and [014](../../plans/cad-backend-replacement/issues/014-vendor-mesh-collision-validation.md) carry this boundary.

The [OCCT comparison handoff](../../../.evidence/claude-occt-gate-compare/HANDOFF.md) motivates a bounded experiment on the nine mesh-only failures at product 0.1 mm/0.5 rad settings, using authenticated per-context precision and recording displacement/completeness. Its comparison reports OCCT mesh defects on 10 of 17 failing files and no geometry loss on the eight where Monstertruck loses source geometry. Different tessellation settings and unresolved face-occurrence/aggregation differences prevent treating those counts as like-for-like accuracy evidence. Declared precision is a repair hypothesis to test, not a proved explanation for every failure. Historical strict-gate counts below remain unchanged; no new asset acceptance count or 0.01 mm qualification is claimed.

The full goal is verified production integration of a complete Monstertruck replacement for Cadrum, preserving intended features and accuracy and removing OCCT/C++ from CAD production, build, packaging and required tests. Completion requires the entire feature assessment in current production code, required native/WASM/browser checks, comparable performance measurements and actual dependency/build/packaged-artifact audits. Qualification milestones do not complete the goal.

`chief-of-staff` coordinates the goal through the existing skills: `to-tickets` → `tdd` → `code-review` → integration and acceptance checks. Use the [approved CAD ticket map](../../plans/cad-backend-replacement/map.md) and [existing tracker conventions](../../agents/issue-tracker.md), with independently verifiable slices and explicit blocking edges. Reconcile current work into those slices without replacing unrelated plans or creating another task register, dashboard or orchestration framework.

Preserve agents, owned changes, dirty work and frozen evidence. Continue in-scope layered construction, angular/source-completeness, vendor import and case/PCB export assignments without restart. Stand down exact keycap/dish and general Boolean hardening, freezing their state; the same keycap owner carries reusable fixtures into009's additive mesh candidate. Assign disjoint writable scope and keep one integration owner. Prefer fresh contexts for new tasks, default Luna Max Fast, and Sol6.1 High/XHigh Fast when needed; no new Astra. Escalation carries forward stable handoffs.

API adaptation and legacy bug fixes are authorized where they improve the replacement. Preserve intended features and valid geometry, rather than Cadrum implementation quirks; update affected callers and regression tests together and document observable behavior changes. The private Plane repair has a specifically reviewed arithmetic-order boundary: authored geometry is evaluated before its retained affine transform, correcting the loss of common-plane identity from independently rounded world corners. Promotion requires exact new-order and legacy serialized/no-transform pins plus serialization compatibility evidence; this does not authorize unrelated saved-arithmetic changes.

Build slices with TDD at agreed public seams and independent expected results. Existing experiments remain evidence with their stated limits; do not relabel helper simulations as production regressions. A handoff identifies the exact source/diff, behavior, executed checks and unresolved risks. Review that recorded scope and review subsequent changed files before integration. Reuse sufficient evidence; rerun affected checks on the exact combined tree when integration or unresolved risks require it. Coordinate on readiness, blocker and completion updates instead of repeated status polling.

A ticket closes only on its own acceptance criteria. The goal completes only after full feature, STEP, accuracy, cache and browser acceptance plus the final dependency/build/artifact removal audit. Bounded experiments, passing unit tests and successful compilation do not substitute for those requirements. The runner-hardening experiment stays preserved and unpromoted; additional tooling requires a demonstrated gap in the existing workflow.
