# Spec review: `97a779a6`

## Findings

- **[P2] Failed exports remain registered in the session.** The session removes an export from `self.exports` after `ExportFinished`, but the `ExportFailed` arm only settles it ([`application/src/session.rs`](/home/chris/.local/share/boardstudio/worktrees/migration-m1-continuation-20261001/application/src/session.rs:886)). Every failed archive/STEP export therefore retains its operation, scope, and snapshot token for the lifetime of the session; later cancellation revisits already-settled operations. Remove the matching entry on failure. This conflicts with the session spec’s requirement that “Every waiter settles on completion/cancel/failure/close” and its bounded resource/lifecycle requirements.
