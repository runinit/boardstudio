## Existing path and test seams

`PartsPreviewPanel` in `web/src/presentation/parts/preview.rs` owns the visible **2D footprint** / **3D model** buttons and keeps the sample form wrapper outside the viewer. A current sample becomes `PartsSampleViewer`, which mounts `CaseSharedViewer` from `web/src/presentation/shared_viewer.rs`. `SharedViewer` creates its canvas and directly awaits `RendererPageHost::mount(...)`; on init error it sets status to an alert and emits a scoped `ViewerSignalKind::Failed`. For Parts, the receiver in `PartsSampleViewer` checks source identity/currentness and calls `runtime.report`. The panel toggle and parent mechanical-fit controls are outside the renderer host's lifetime.

There is no renderer factory/provider in `SharedViewer` or `Runtime`: `RendererPageHost::mount` is a direct static call. Existing `scripted_renderer("setScene")` in `renderer_host_page_base.rs` reaches the real `RendererHost::attach` initialization cleanup path, but it is private to host lifecycle tests and cannot drive the mounted `SharedViewer` status branch. The Keycaps fixture `presentation::layout_viewer::mounted_keycaps_preview_tests::mounted_preview_failure_retries_and_ignores_a_late_old_owner_reply` is the best mounted pattern: real `VirtualDom`, controlled async provider, rendered alert assertion, retry, owner change, stale reply rejection. It does not inject renderer initialization.
